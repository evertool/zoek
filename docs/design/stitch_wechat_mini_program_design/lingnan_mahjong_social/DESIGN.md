## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the communal, lively, and warm spirit of traditional Lingnan mahjong parlors, translated into an ultra-clean, modern WeChat Mini Program interface. The design balances authentic mahjong heritage with contemporary mobile product design, creating an approachable, tactile digital tabletop where friends gather effortlessly.

### Visual Personality & Aesthetic Movement
The aesthetic direction is **Tactile Modernism** rooted in mahjong material culture:
- **Ivory & Melamine Finishes**: Surface containers mimic the smooth, rounded, dense heft of physical mahjong tiles rather than flat digital rectangles. Subtle multi-layer ambient highlights and warm ivory tones give cards a distinct physical presence.
- **Classic Lingnan Tablecloth Backdrop**: A rich, deep felt emerald green (`#1B6B4A`) anchors the canvas, evoking the timeless atmosphere of felt mahjong mats, accented by crisp ivory tiles, celebratory cinnabar red, and winner's gold.
- **WeChat Mini Program Native Harmony**: Built specifically for the 750rpx layout grid, respecting iPhone home indicator bars, native 4-item bottom tab bars, and the mandatory top-right capsule button safe zones.
- **Playful Cantonese Soul**: Visual hierarchy prioritizes expressive Cantonese vernacular slogans and immediate numeric legibility, balancing lighthearted street-corner wit with pristine scorekeeping clarity.

## Colors

The color system draws directly from the physical elements of the mahjong table: the deep felt mat, the two-tone layered acrylic and bone tiles, and the carved cinnabar lacquer characters.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#1B6B4A`)**: Tabletop Felt Green. Used for primary brand moments, major confirmation buttons, active tab states, and positive score indications (`+16`).
- **Secondary (`#D83A34`)**: Traditional Mahjong "Red Middle" (紅中) Cinnabar. Used for negative points, critical alert triggers, urgent player-shortage badges, and celebratory victory seals.
- **Tertiary (`#F2A900`)**: Winner's Antique Brass & Gold. Reserved strictly for 1st place finishes, "雀神" badges, streak flames, and crown awards.
- **Neutral & Surfaces**:
  - `mahjong-mat-deep` (`#0E3E2B`) and `mahjong-mat-surface` (`#145238`): Immersive dark felt backgrounds for room lobby headers and settlement hero zones.
  - `tile-ivory` (`#FAF8F2`) and `surface-layer-1` (`#FFFFFF`): Warm, dense tile surfaces for cards and modals.
  - `text-primary` (`#1A201C`): Charcoal-pine ink providing high contrast and comfortable readability against ivory and white backgrounds.

### Color Semantics in Scorekeeping
- **Positive Deltas / Wins**: Displayed in `positive-score` (`#1B6B4A`) with an explicit leading `+` glyph.
- **Negative Deltas / Losses**: Rendered in `negative-score` (`#D83A34`) with a leading `-` glyph.
- **Zero-Sum Equilibrium Balance**: Neutral slate when round totals balance to exactly `0`, turning into `cinnabar-red` with an alert indicator whenever input totals fail zero-sum verification.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes rapid glanceability during fast-paced physical mahjong matches. 

### Font Selection & Pairing
- **Headlines & Scores (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Modern, geometric, and open. Selected for its rounded counters and punchy bold weights. Numeric characters have clear, distinct silhouettes to avoid score confusion at arm's length.
- **Body & Slogans (`Be Vietnam Pro`)**: Balanced and friendly with wide apertures, ensuring dense Cantonese characters (like 唔, 嘅, 翻, 糊, 雀) remain clear and legible on smaller mobile screens.

### Numerical Hierarchy
- Scores in game entry use the dedicated `score-numeral-lg` token (48px / 96rpx) paired with an oversized `+` or `-` sign, allowing players across the physical table to confirm values at a glance.
- Dynamic Cantonese slogans use `headline-md` and `headline-sm` with tight vertical metrics so floating banners stay punchy and compact.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout adheres to the WeChat Mini Program 750rpx viewport standard (375pt base width on standard Apple retina viewports).

### Layout Grid & Safe Margins
- **Horizontal Screen Padding**: `32rpx` on both lateral borders, establishing a standard 686rpx working width for primary cards and interaction panels.
- **Top Bar Safe Area**:
  - `navigationStyle: custom` is active across all views.
  - The status bar and native WeChat capsule button occupy the top header area.
  - A minimum top offset of `88rpx` (`safe-capsule-padding-top`) clears the status bar, and interactive header actions maintain a right inset margin of `200rpx` (`safe-capsule-right`) to prevent collisions with the native triple-dot and close capsule.
- **Bottom TabBar Safe Area**:
  - All scroll views must append `112rpx` padding-bottom (`safe-tabbar-bottom`) on tab pages to clear the native bottom bar plus iPhone gesture indicator.

### Rhythm & Vertical Stacking
- Component gaps default to `24rpx` (`space-md`) for related elements inside cards.
- Major card blocks and timeline items are spaced `32rpx` (`space-lg`) apart.
- Touch targets strictly maintain a minimum tap height of `88rpx` (44px) to accommodate rapid, casual tapping during physical play.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **Tactile Tile Elevation**, pairing ivory melamine card layers with soft, dual-tinted ambient shadows inspired by physical tiles resting on a green baize tablecloth.

### Elevation Levels

1. **Table Mat Level (Base Canvas / Level 0)**:
   - Deep felt green (`#145238` or neutral paper tint `#F4F5F2`).
   - Completely flat with no shadow.

2. **Tile Card Level (Level 1 - Regular Cards & Row Items)**:
   - Surface: Warm Ivory (`#FAF8F2` or `#FFFFFF`).
   - Outline: 1rpx solid `#EDE7DA`.
   - Shadow: `0 4rpx 12rpx rgba(14, 62, 43, 0.08), 0 1rpx 2rpx rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
   - Mimics a single mahjong tile lying flat on the table mat.

3. **Active Tile / Standing Hand Level (Level 2 - Score Keyboards, Modals, Quick Actions)**:
   - Surface: Pure Tile Ivory (`#FFFFFF`).
   - Shadow: `0 12rpx 32rpx rgba(14, 62, 43, 0.16), 0 2rpx 6rpx rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`.
   - Outline: 2rpx solid `#E2D9C8` with a subtle top edge highlight (`inset 0 1rpx 0 #FFFFFF`).
   - Conveys the tactile weight of a standing tile or raised game piece.

4. **Floating Toast & Slogan Animation Layer (Level 3 - Lottie Modals)**:
   - Background Dim: `rgba(14, 32, 23, 0.65)` with light backdrop blur (`8px`).
   - Modal Container: High-relief shadow (`0 24rpx 64rpx rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.28)`), presenting the floating slogan as the focal hero element.

## Shapes

The design language uses roundedness level `2` (8px / 16rpx base radius), echoing the rounded chamfers and milled edges of real mahjong tiles.

### Geometric Rules
- **Base Components (Inputs, Small Tags, Badges)**: `16rpx` (0.5rem) border radius, giving them a tactile, pill-soft feel without becoming full semicircles.
- **Cards, Sheets & Containers (`rounded-lg`)**: `32rpx` (1.0rem) border radius. Matches the corner curve of standard Chinese tournament tiles.
- **Hero Modals & Floating Slogan Pods (`rounded-xl`)**: `48rpx` (1.5rem) border radius, conveying a soft, welcoming, and relaxed social atmosphere.
- **Pill Badges & Avatar Frames**: Semicircular (`9999rpx`) for counters, player status tags ("台主", "你"), and bottom floating CTA pills.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (开台 / 确认锁定 / 提交积分)**:
  - Background: Primary felt green (`#1B6B4A`).
  - Text: High-contrast white (`#FFFFFF`), `label-lg`, center-aligned.
  - Height: `96rpx`. Border-radius: `24rpx`.
  - Tactile Detail: Bottom shadow `0 6rpx 0 #0E3E2B` (creates a physical push-button effect that compresses by `4rpx` on active press).
- **Secondary Action (修改积分 / 补分退分)**:
  - Background: Warm ivory (`#FAF8F2`), border `2rpx solid #D8CFBC`.
  - Text: Pine neutral (`#1A201C`), `label-lg`.
- **Destructive / Exit Button (散台 / 取消牌台)**:
  - Subtle text button or tinted cinnabar wash (`#D83A34` at 10% fill with `#D83A34` text).

### 2. The Mahjong Score Card (牌局计分卡)
- Rendered as an ivory tile block.
- Top section holds the player avatar (circular with 4rpx ivory border), Cantonese nickname, and role chip ("台主" in amber gold, "你" in emerald green).
- Center section displays the score entry bracket: framed in a recessed score input container with inset shadows, highlighting point values in 48px `score-numeral-lg`. Positive scores automatically show a green `+` pill; negative scores display a cinnabar `-` pill.

### 3. Floating Cantonese Slogan Toast (Lottie Container)
- Appears centered or as a top floating bubble for 2.5s–3s.
- Container: Ivory finish (`#FFFFFF`) with 48rpx rounded corners and a 3rpx cinnabar red accent border on win states, or calm emerald border on waiting states.
- Structure: Centered Lottie animation (max 50KB JSON) at 160rpx × 160rpx, followed directly by the bold Cantonese slogan text in `headline-md`.

### 4. Zero-Sum Verification Bar (核对平衡器)
- Sticky above the confirmation button.
- When `Sum == 0`: Pill turns soft green (`#E8F4EC`), icon shows a checkmark, text reads: `「分数平衡，可以锁定」`.
- When `Sum != 0`: Pill turns urgent cinnabar tint (`#FDECEB`), text displays difference: `「相差 ${diff} 分，请雀友核对」`. Submit button disables automatically.

### 5. Settlement Pods & Badges (雀神 / 胜场徽章)
- Ranks 1 to 4 arranged with medal color accents:
  - Rank 1: Winner's Antique Gold (`#F2A900`) crown badge and gold score typography.
  - Rank 2: Frosted Silver (`#8E9B93`).
  - Rank 3: Warm Bronze (`#C28456`).
  - Rank 4: Soft Slate (`#6B7A72`).
- Badges (`雀神`, `连胜王`, `大翻盘`, `稳如泰山`) are displayed as embossed tile miniatures with high-contrast colored iconography and compact text tags beneath the user profile card.
